1. Executive Summary & Problem Space

Sialkot, Pakistan accounts for over 70% of global production of surgical instruments. However, Pakistani exporters suffer recurring, devastating shipment detentions at US ports under FDA Import Alert 76-01 ("Detention Without Physical Examination of Surgical Instruments from Specified Pakistani Manufacturers").

The core issue is post-manufacturing discovery: small-to-medium factories accept overseas Purchase Orders (POs) without pre-verifying if their technical quality documentation meets US statutory mandates. They discover critical non-compliances (such as unvalidated passivation baths under 21 CFR 820.75 or non-compliant stainless steel chemistry under ASTM F899) only after the cargo lands at US ports or when foreign inspectors arrive.

Core Objective: "ExportReady" provides a multi-tenant decision-support platform that transforms passive compliance documents into a cryptographically verified, stochastically scheduled, and regulation-enforced export pipeline.

4. Mathematical Formulations

4.1 Cryptographic Ledger Chaining & Backdating Detection

For document $i$ at sequence $k$ within factory tenant $T$:

Payload_k = SHA256(File_k) || DocType_k || ClaimedDate_k || UploadTimestamp_UTC
H_k = SHA256(H_{k-1} || Payload_k)   where H_0 = SHA256("GENESIS_BLOCK_" || TenantID)

Backdating Heuristic:
Delta_Days = |UploadDate - ClaimedDate|
If Delta_Days > 30 days => Status = TAMPER_SUSPECT_BACKDATED => Downgrade to SELF_DECLARED

4.2 Monte Carlo Lead Time Risk Simulation (PERT Distribution)

Each open compliance gap $g$ has three-point duration estimates: minimum $a_g$, most likely $m_g$, and maximum $b_g$:

Mean: mu_g = (a_g + 4*m_g + b_g) / 6
Standard Deviation: sigma_g = (b_g - a_g) / 6
Beta Parameters: alpha_g = 1 + 4*((m_g - a_g)/(b_g - a_g)),  beta_g = 1 + 4*((b_g - m_g)/(b_g - a_g))
Sample Draw: X_g ~ a_g + (b_g - a_g) * Beta(alpha_g, beta_g)

Across 10,000 iterations:
Total_Duration^(j) = SUM(X_g^(j))
P(on-time) = (1 / 10000) * SUM( [Total_Duration^(j) <= (Deadline - IntakeDate)] )
